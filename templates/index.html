How to Fix Camera Access:
  1. Check Browser URL: Ensure you are opening http://localhost:5000/user or http://127.0.0.1:5000/user. (Do not use an IP address like http://192.x.x.x:5000 as browsers disable cameras on insecure IPs).
  2. Browser Site Permissions: Click the tune/lock icon on the left side of your browser address bar (next to the URL), find Camera, and change it from Block to Allow. Then refresh the page.
  3. Windows Camera Privacy: Open Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Camera. Make sure "Camera access" and "Let desktop apps access your camera" are toggled ON.
  4. Close Other Apps: If Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Skype, or another browser tab is using your webcam, close them and click "Start Camera" again.
