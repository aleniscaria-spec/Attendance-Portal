How to Fix Camera Access:
  1. Check URL: Make sure you are visiting http://localhost:5000/register or http://127.0.0.1:5000/register.
  2. Browser Permission: Click the tune/lock icon in the browser address bar (next to the URL) → set Camera to Allow → Refresh page (F5).
  3. Windows Settings: Open Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → Ensure "Camera access" and "Let desktop apps access your camera" are turned ON.
  4. Quick Workaround: Click "Upload Image" to enroll student face images directly from your computer!
Registration Requirements:
  • Exactly ONE face must be visible in the camera frame.
  • Ensure good ambient lighting and avoid dark shadows or glare.
  • Face forward without sunglasses or masks covering features.
